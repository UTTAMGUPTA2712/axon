What it does

Three things, all of them about the tab you already have open.

Reads the page

Ask a question and Axon pulls the page's text, or takes a screenshot when the answer is visual, and sends only that to the model you chose. It can transcribe a chart, work through an error trace, or cut a long thread down to its argument.

Fills forms

Axon matches form fields to details you have saved by meaning rather than by name, so a field labelled "Mobile number" is filled from a fact you saved as "Phone". It reaches inside iframes and works on forms React and Vue render after load. It never fills passwords, card numbers or one-time codes, and never uses details you saved about someone else.

Remembers what you tell it

Mention your role, a portfolio link or how you like things worded, and Axon keeps it — in your browser — so the next form and the next draft already know. You can read and edit every stored detail in settings, and delete any of them.

Where your keys and your data go

There is no Axon server. You bring a key from a provider you already trust, Axon keeps it in Chrome's own chrome.storage.sync, and requests go from your browser straight to that provider. Nothing is proxied, logged or kept by us, because there is no "us" in the request path at all.

Your API keyHeld in chrome.storage.sync, sent only to the provider you configured

ScreenshotsTaken only when you invoke Axon, and only of the tab you are on
Your profileStays in your browser; you can read, edit and delete every entry in settings
AnalyticsNone. There is no telemetry and no third-party script

Prefer nothing leave your machine at all? Point the custom provider at Ollama or LM Studio and Axon will talk to a model running locally.

Questions

Is Axon free to use?

Yes. Axon is free and open-source. You bring your own API key from a provider such as Google Gemini, which has a generous free tier, Groq, or OpenAI. You can also point it at a local model through any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, which costs nothing to run.

Where are my API keys stored?

In your browser, in Chrome's own chrome.storage.sync area. There is no Axon server: keys are sent only to the provider endpoint you configured, directly from your browser.

Which AI models does Axon support?

Google Gemini, Groq, and OpenAI are built in, and a custom provider works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including Ollama and LM Studio running on your own machine. You can assign a different model to chat, vision, intent routing and memory.

Does Axon track my browsing history or screen data?

No. Axon reads the page or captures the screen only when you invoke it, through the toolbar icon, a keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu. It requests no browsing-history permission, and Chrome grants it access to a page only for that visit.
